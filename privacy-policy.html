Dipa Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Thank you for using Dipa ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Dipa is designed to help you turn guided reflection into personal affirmations and practical if-then plans. The App may process the information you choose to enter during a reflection so it can provide these features.

We may also receive limited, non-personal technical information needed to operate and improve the App, such as device or app version information and general usage events.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Dipa’s reflection, affirmation, and reminder features.
  • To personalize the content generated from your own reflections.
  • To diagnose technical issues and understand general product usage.

3. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. You should also use the security features available on your device and keep the App up to date.

4. Third-Party Services

Dipa may rely on Apple services and other service providers needed to deliver features such as notifications, app distribution, and payments. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

Dipa is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Dipa handles information, please contact Elena Muntian through the support contact provided in the App Store listing or within the App.

8. Acceptance

By using Dipa, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
